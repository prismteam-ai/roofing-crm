# Known limitations

The CRM is a source-backed evidence workspace, not a guarantee of complete county coverage or a verified roofing-sales opportunity. The original [assignment](history/ASSIGNMENT_README.md) remains controlling; a safe empty-result path does not fulfill an unavailable positive source outcome.

## Final free-source disposition

The completed finite lawful free-source campaign did not establish a qualified positive **current-active or long-open roofing-permit cohort**. This remains a material functional limitation; it is not proof that no such Oakland permit exists. Native `ISSUED` is not `OPEN`. An old issue date or missing final date cannot establish active status or uninterrupted open duration. Historical recent Troy observations remain issued-only.

No paid source is required, purchased or needed to reproduce the submitted runtime. Paid full history remains unsearched and unknown; it is not a prerequisite in the original assignment. Acquisition is closed for release assembly, and no new subscription, source search or synthetic positive is used to conceal this gap.

## Claim strength and coverage

- County permit/enrichment coverage is fragmented. Completeness requires a measured denominator, observation window and lawful-access boundary.
- Building year is a low-confidence roof-age proxy, not a roof installation/replacement date or condition claim.
- Five bounded current-PIN deed-index gap observations and partial sale events do not establish complete title or uninterrupted legal ownership continuity. Predecessor lineage and observation-window qualifications remain visible.
- Permit-listed applicants/businesses, deterministic matches, contractors and verified state licensees are different roles. Available license observations do not by themselves verify a property's contractor.
- BBB remains unavailable in approved reusable data; absent ratings are not zero.
- Raw/provider intake, owner/contact data and operational records are not public IPFS, unrestricted download or screenshot material. Protected views require the low-privilege review session.

## Release and external gates

The accepted dated runtime is identified in [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.json). A later source commit needs a source-independent final deployment/acceptance receipt; health or local CI alone is insufficient. Local CI parity is not an executed GitHub-hosted Actions run, and finite checks do not guarantee no future bugs.

Historical public CIDs and independent retrieval do not establish full current Atlas publication, registry/IPNS/on-chain mutation or shared MCP synchronization. These remain separate unperformed external stages. Local media/PR preparation does not perform video upload, GitHub writes, Ready transition, employer messaging or official submission.

AWS resources have finite retention, quota and usage costs. Neither perpetual availability nor zero future charge is promised. The [evaluator guide](EVALUATOR_GUIDE.md) gives dated expiry/quota facts and recovery; the [cost model](COST_MODEL.md) separates measured usage, credits, reserves and forecasts.
