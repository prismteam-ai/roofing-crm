# Known limitations

The CRM is a source-backed evidence workspace, not a guarantee of complete county coverage or a verified roofing-sales opportunity. The original [assignment](history/ASSIGNMENT_README.md) remains controlling; a safe empty-result path does not fulfill an unavailable positive source outcome.

## Final free-source disposition

The product supports strict source-confirmed open filtering and a separate [status-verification workflow](PERMIT_OPPORTUNITIES.md) using real older roofing observations. The as-of 2026-10-28 full admitted census found 4,487 candidate permits/4,227 properties; two lack mapped points. These are not five-mile counts or confirmed-open records.

The completed finite lawful free-source campaign did not establish a qualified positive **current-active or long-open roofing-permit cohort**. This remains a material functional limitation; it is not proof that no such Oakland permit exists. Native `ISSUED` is not `OPEN`. An old issue date or missing final date cannot establish active status or uninterrupted open duration. Municipal Troy candidate records retain unavailable native status as `reportedStatus:null` with `issued_only` basis, rather than a fabricated `ISSUED` label. Other sources may report `ISSUED` literally without establishing OPEN.

No paid source is required, purchased or needed to reproduce the submitted runtime. Paid full history remains unsearched and unknown; it is not a prerequisite in the original assignment. Acquisition is closed for release assembly, and no new subscription, source search or synthetic positive is used to conceal this gap.

The original 114-row trace, 62 null / 52 false historical flags and eight source-positive gaps remain unchanged. A positive follow-up cohort does not close the missing confirmed-open or ownership-history outcomes.

## Claim strength and coverage

- County permit/enrichment coverage is fragmented. Completeness requires a measured denominator, observation window and lawful-access boundary.
- Building year is a low-confidence roof-age proxy, not a roof installation/replacement date or condition claim.
- Five bounded current-PIN deed-index gap observations and partial sale events do not establish complete title or uninterrupted legal ownership continuity. Predecessor lineage and observation-window qualifications remain visible.
- Permit-listed applicants/businesses, deterministic matches, contractors and verified state licensees are different roles. Available license observations do not by themselves verify a property's contractor.
- BBB remains unavailable in approved reusable data; absent ratings are not zero.
- Official permitted owner/postal/property facts may appear in evaluator UI/media. Source-restricted provider raw material, actual secrets and mutable user state keep their specific boundaries. Bulk public publication needs a field-level candidate and exact-byte Human approval; the historical owner-free profile is not an acquisition/display prohibition.

The accepted dated runtime is identified in [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.json). Local CI parity is not an executed GitHub-hosted Actions run, and finite checks do not guarantee no future bugs.

Historical public CIDs and independent retrieval do not establish full current Atlas publication, registry/IPNS/on-chain mutation or shared MCP synchronization. These remain separate unperformed external stages. Local media/PR preparation does not perform video upload, GitHub writes, Ready transition, employer messaging or official submission.

The [evaluator guide](EVALUATOR_GUIDE.md) gives dated expiry/quota facts and recovery; the [cost model](COST_MODEL.md) separates measured usage, credits, reserves and forecasts.
