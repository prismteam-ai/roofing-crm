# Known limitations

This file describes factual boundaries, not permission to weaken the assessment. The release return supplies current source-campaign dispositions and exact evidence identities.

- County permit and enrichment coverage is fragmented. Completeness must be stated with its measured denominator and lawful-access boundary.
- Native issued-only status does not prove current openness. Long-open claims require a supported start date and explicit active status as of a justified observation date.
- Building year is not a roof replacement date or roof condition.
- Partial sale events and qualified no-recorded-deed observations do not establish complete title or uninterrupted ownership continuity.
- Permit-listed business/applicant roles, deterministic business matches and verified state licenses are distinct.
- BBB data remains unavailable unless lawful reuse permission exists; missing ratings are not zero.
- Provider/raw intake cannot be redistributed through public IPFS, downloads, Git, screenshots or an unrestricted API.
- Internal metric/dashboard registries and external publication/organization actions remain unperformed without access and authorization.
- Local CI parity is not an executed GitHub-hosted Actions run. Final PR/video/Ready/submission gates remain separate.
- AWS review stacks have retention and usage cost; neither perpetual availability nor zero future charge is promised. Existing spending ceilings remain enforced.
