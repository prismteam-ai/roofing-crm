# Product mission

Help a roofing sales rep, in a defined service area, turn nearby properties into a day’s worth of doors worth knocking — without guessing.

## The job

When I’m working a territory (walking, driving, or planning from a desk), I want to see which nearby properties look like good roofing opportunities — aging roofs and long-open permits — so I can build today’s lead list and convert the ones worth outreach.

- **Functional:** Find candidates in a radius, inspect the facts, save leads.
- **Emotional:** Feel confident I’m knocking the _right_ doors, not random ones.
- **Social:** Show the team I worked the territory with evidence, not vibes.

## Progress we exist to make

**From:** Spreadsheets, memory, and blind canvassing. Forty-five minutes on a block and no way to see which roofs are old or which permits have sat open for years.

**To:** Pin or GPS, a radius, a scannable map and list, enough property and contractor context to judge the door, and a lead record that is still there when I come back.

The product is hired at the struggling moment — _which streets do I work next?_ — not at “manage a CRM.” Identification and conversion come first. Everything else on the chrome is a later job.

## What we will not pretend to be

We consume property, permit, and enrichment data that already exists. We do not ingest it. We do not message owners. We do not treat a missing county as covered geography.

Users often lose to **nothing**: staying with paper lists and gut feel is safer than trusting a map they don’t believe. Trust in place, signals, and saved leads has to beat that inertia before we add more surfaces.
