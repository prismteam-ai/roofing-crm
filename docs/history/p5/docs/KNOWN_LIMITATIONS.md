# Known limitations

- Acquisition is partial across Oakland sources. A 490,799-ID public baseline
  and 63-feature place inventory do not establish complete permit enrichment.
- The current Independence cohort has literal CLOSED roof observations and zero
  canonical OPEN permits. Issued, finaled, expired and other statuses do not
  silently become open/closed. Missing lifecycle dates prevent duration claims.
- Building-year data is low-confidence roof-age context, not a verified roof
  replacement. Only-unknown roof filtering lacks a distinct typed predicate.
- Recorded owner mailing-state classes are not current residence or city-local
  identity. Partial reported sales cannot establish uninterrupted tenure.
- Troy reported applicant/license attachments do not verify contractor role or
  active license eligibility. Missing verified company and BBB evidence remain
  conditional unknowns. Numeric/grade rating filters are unsupported.
- Place resolution covers eligible official Oakland records only. Ambiguous or
  quarantined identities refuse. A center-radius search is not polygon
  containment. Non-mile units and unsupported language require clarification.
- The compositional parser has a finite grammar. Unrecognized material text is
  refused, and false refusals remain possible. The corpus and mutation checks
  are bounded evidence, not a universal natural-language correctness proof.
- The model context may fit fewer rows than the returned page. Selection is a
  bounded presentation, not complete match coverage. Empty and fallback modes
  are not model successes.
- Historical CID1/CID2 represent an assessment product snapshot. Local Elephant
  validation/hash/export or an unmerged Atlas page is not global publication.
- Local/unit/emulator/browser fixtures do not substitute for clean final hosted
  tests, actual concurrency reads, source rights checks, live-model accounting,
  security review, independent review or evaluator first-use validation.
- No final media recording/upload or remote Git publication is permitted until
  the applicable direct Human and review gates are satisfied.

Rochester Hills source-labeled Owner observations are neither current legal-owner
verification nor ten-year tenure proof. Applied reports are not issued permits.
Printed PDF record/cost totals have documented discrepancies; the source values
and independently fenced record rows are retained without forcing reconciliation.

The private v7 extension retains all eight v6 data files byte for byte and adds
one official Troy Real Property Assessing Report case observed through the normal
public UI. All 39 displayed fields remain in its protected source table, with
manual-transcription provenance; there is no captured server body or bulk-source
claim. Explicit Owner Name 1/2 and Owner mailing fields are private observations,
with no known ownership-effective date or related permit event. These fields
remain excluded from model input and public publication.

This assessment association uses one exact normalized printed parcel number and
exact CITY OF TROY jurisdiction. Source and county street text differ: no number-
word/suffix equivalence or fuzzy address match is asserted. The source association
is medium confidence and does not verify a building, unit, roof, residence or
current legal ownership. Owner and roof views retain that limitation. The
separate owner-mailing-to-site comparison stays unknown. A reported Owner State
of MI supplies one spatially usable regional/in-state mailing observation with
source provenance, not a county-local or residence conclusion.

The source commercial-building summary year 2022 adds one low-confidence
building-year proxy. The raw slash-formatted sale string in 2012 has no observed format label:
the normalized sale date remains null, and the raw source value is retained. It
cannot prove uninterrupted tenure; the source also describes a later parcel split.
The local v7 proof reconciles 3,747 owner observations (3,746 prior Rochester
observations unchanged) and 39,282 normalized permit observations unchanged.
One current source case is not municipal coverage or evidence of source exhaustion.
