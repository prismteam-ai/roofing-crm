# Data coverage, evidence and rights

Release-bound counts, snapshot IDs, digests and observation dates come from the admitted Oracle manifest, health, coverage and source reports in the accompanying review return. Historical counts must not be relabeled as a new source refresh. The CRM does not widen a lawful source boundary to obtain a positive demo.

Public artifacts contain approved public-safe county parcel/address facts only. Provider observations and minimized official evidence are protected authenticated projections; raw source/provider intake remains private. No bulk provider export, raw owner/contact intake or unrestricted source download is exposed through the CRM.

- Native permit status is retained. `ISSUED` alone does not establish current OPEN status.
- Open duration requires an explicit active status and a justified start/observation date.
- Building year is a low-confidence age proxy, not a roof installation or condition claim.
- A reported sale or a no-recorded-deed observation is not proven uninterrupted ownership continuity.
- Permit-listed applicant/business, matched business, contractor and verified licensee remain distinct roles.
- BBB absence is unavailable/not observed; it is never converted to a zero rating.

A positive source workflow is accepted only when the source receipts, coverage window and exact joined records support it. [Known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) states the current limitations; [evaluator guide](EVALUATOR_GUIDE.md) describes how to inspect provenance and unknowns.
