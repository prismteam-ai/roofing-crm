# Data coverage, evidence and rights

Release-bound counts, snapshot IDs, digests and observation dates come from the admitted Oracle manifest, health, coverage and source reports in the accompanying review return. Historical counts must not be relabeled as a new source refresh. The CRM does not widen a lawful source boundary to obtain a positive demo.

Historical public artifacts contain the fields selected for those immutable releases. Runtime observations use the bounded authenticated read plane, an operational transport choice. Official source-permitted facts may be visible; provider-restricted raw material keeps its own source-specific limits. Raw source captures and bulk working payloads remain outside Git. The CRM consumes admitted observations and does not become a bulk source-download or ingestion service.

- Native permit status is retained. `ISSUED` alone does not establish current OPEN status.
- Open duration requires an explicit active status and a justified start/observation date.
- Building year is a low-confidence age proxy, not a roof installation or condition claim.
- A reported sale or a no-recorded-deed observation is not proven uninterrupted ownership continuity.
- Permit-listed applicant/business, matched business, contractor and verified licensee remain distinct roles.
- BBB absence is unavailable/not observed; it is never converted to a zero rating.

A positive source workflow is accepted only when the source receipts, coverage window and exact joined records support it. [Known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) states the current limitations; [evaluator guide](EVALUATOR_GUIDE.md) describes how to inspect provenance and unknowns.

The source contract preserves independent municipal permit facts and provider-normalized observations. The additive official parcel observation is separately versioned and validated; producer/consumer manifests and the external release receipt bind the exact current contract and admission. `providerPermitStatus`, `permitActivityClaim` and `minPermitAgeSinceIssueDays` must be satisfied by one observation. An ISSUED-only observation stays activity-not-established; age since issue never becomes continuous open duration. Provider business/party labels and license identifiers remain unverified source observations, with BBB unavailable unless independently acquired.

`minRecordedDeedGapYears` selects only documented current-PIN index observations whose claim window and latest observed deed predate the strict calendar cutoff at that observation's `observedThrough`. A source-wide advertised index start is not a property history window. Current-PIN completeness, verified-through date, retrospective witness qualifications and unreviewed predecessor lineage remain visible. Stronger uninterrupted/legal ownership queries are refused and offer this explicit narrower alternative. Historical public wire artifacts retain their own immutable schema; the producer explicitly validates compatibility before joining the current source-observation graph.

Official source-permitted identities, addresses, coordinates and owner/postal observations are visible in evaluator UI/media. Recording selectors are not authority to suppress public facts. Actually restricted-provider raw material and credentials retain their source-specific/security controls. Model input minimization and body-free telemetry remain operational/grounding choices; server-owned citations retain the exact observation digest.

The original “open roofing permits older than five years” first uses strict source-confirmed lifecycle and source-calendar age from the selected observation. When strict results are empty, shared 0.7.0 supplies a separate **Status verification needed** section at the same age/radius constraints. Its issue/filed age is not uninterrupted work history. Explicit continuous/open-for duration still requires independently documented interval evidence. Both reads retain the selected observation’s own anchor; browser today cannot silently replace it. See [permit opportunities](PERMIT_OPPORTUNITIES.md) for the derived modes, measured full cohort and API examples. Provider-classified ROOFING candidates with a retained municipal `other` classification are shown with that disagreement, without rewriting municipal facts.

The accepted real age example is City of Novi, Michigan, within five miles (8046.72 meters), `roofMinAgeYears=15`, unknown ages excluded. The unchanged operator is **age greater than 15**, using an explicitly labeled building-year proxy. The accepted 2026-10-29 receipt had 648 matches and 50 returned rows. Compare the final promotion receipt before using those counts as current. Troy's historical strict-open empty case remains a separate regression from its positive verification-candidate cohort; the original city placeholder does not require the permit and aged-roof examples to use one place.

The completed lawful free-source permit campaign remains the permit-acquisition boundary. No paid plan is required, purchased or needed to reproduce the runtime; paid full history remains unsearched and unknown. The bounded official field census and retained-row official observation sidecar do not refresh that permit campaign or rewrite historical v8/public bytes. See [known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) for the single source-outcome disposition; a redeploy is not a source refresh or proof of countywide completeness.
