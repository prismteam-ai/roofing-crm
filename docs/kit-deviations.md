# Deviations from the soofi-xyz team kit

The two repositories (this one and the Roofing CRM) follow the kit's Golden Path. The kit is pinned at commit
71b8341. The rows below are where they do not, and why. Kit rules are cited as `<skill>/<file>` and its
section heading at that commit. "Where" names the repository and file the deviation lives in. The same table is in both
repositories.
