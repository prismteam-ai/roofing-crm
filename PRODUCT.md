## Platform

web

## Users

Roofing sales reps, desk-first on a wide map, same job collapsed to phone sheets in the field.

## Product Purpose

Help a rep, in a defined service area, turn nearby properties into a day’s worth of doors worth knocking — without guessing.

Success: they know where they are, how far they are looking, which doors to consider, convert one with confirm, and find it on the leads list.

## Positioning

Lead identification over already-published Elephant parcels. Honest geography. Aging roofs and long-open permits are signals to inspect, not claims the product invents.

## Operating Context

One Operate workspace. Hierarchy from [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md): map + pin/GPS + radius dominate; list twins the map; drawer judges one door; leads are the receipt; agent and future CRM chrome stay quiet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Never label another county as Chester. Never invent roof age, BBB, or permit rows.
- Consume published data only. No ingest UI. No outbound owner messaging.
- Convert is deliberate and recoverable. Structure year is a proxy.

## Evidence on Hand

- [docs/mission.md](docs/mission.md), [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md), [README.md](README.md)

## Product Principles

1. Hierarchy is the product. Clutter that fights P0–P4 is a failed redesign.
2. Name the county you are actually serving.
3. Signals to inspect, not facts the UI invents.
4. Convert is a write; confirm and archive.
